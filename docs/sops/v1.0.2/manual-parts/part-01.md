## HOW TO USE / كيفية الاستخدام

### How to read this manual / كيفية قراءة الدليل

**English:** Every SOP has the same 16 numbered sections. Begin with the control header, verify you are using the correct SOP ID and version, then follow its prerequisites, procedure, stop conditions and evidence requirements. The English instructions come from the inspected knowledge base and earlier draft; the paired Arabic is a controlled-language working adaptation requiring specialist linguistic review.

**العربية:** يتكون كل إجراء من 16 قسماً مرقماً. ابدأ ببيانات ضبط الإجراء، ثم تحقق من رقم الإجراء والإصدار، وراجع المتطلبات المسبقة وخطوات التنفيذ وشروط التوقف والسجلات المطلوبة. النص العربي صياغة تشغيلية أولية تحتاج مراجعة واعتماداً لغوياً متخصصاً.

### How to use an SOP in a shift / كيفية استخدام الإجراء أثناء المناوبة

**English:** Identify the activity and find its SOP ID in the index. Verify your user, academy and permission. Read the stop conditions before acting. Follow the numbered steps, check the actual system result, capture required record IDs, and hand unresolved items to the named supervisor. Do not treat an SOP as permission to override the system.

**العربية:** حدد النشاط وابحث عن رقم الإجراء في الفهرس. تحقق من حسابك والأكاديمية والصلاحيات. اقرأ شروط التوقف قبل التنفيذ. اتبع الخطوات المرقمة وتحقق من النتيجة الفعلية واحفظ المراجع المطلوبة وسلّم الحالات غير المحسومة إلى المسؤول المحدد. لا يمنحك الإجراء الحق في تجاوز قيود النظام.

### Evidence labels / تصنيفات المعلومات

**English:** CONFIRMED SYSTEM RULE: observed in inspected implementation or tests. OWNER-CONFIRMED CONTEXT: stated by the operator. PROPOSED CONTROL: a suggested human workflow, form or responsibility, not a confirmed screen. TBC — MANAGEMENT APPROVAL REQUIRED: a policy, authority or threshold the evidence does not establish.

**العربية:** قاعدة نظام مؤكدة: سلوك تم التحقق منه في المصدر أو الاختبارات. سياق أكده المشغل: معلومة قدمها مشغل النادي. ضبط مقترح: مسؤولية أو نموذج أو عملية بشرية مقترحة، وليست شاشة مؤكدة. يتطلب اعتماد الإدارة: سياسة أو سلطة أو حد لم تثبته الأدلة.

### Version and approval rules / قواعد الإصدار والاعتماد

**English:** Document version 1.0.0 is the initial consolidated bilingual DRAFT release; it is not an approval or effective date. Proposed semantic versioning: major change for materially restructured process; minor for new approved procedure/form or meaningful addition; patch for corrections/translation fixes. Changes must be reviewed and approved through GOV-001 before an approved edition is issued.

**العربية:** الإصدار 1.0.0 هو أول تجميع ثنائي اللغة بصفة مسودة، ولا يعني اعتماداً أو نفاذاً. الترميز المقترح: الرقم الرئيسي عند إعادة هيكلة مؤثرة؛ الفرعي عند إضافة إجراء أو نموذج؛ ورقم التصحيح لإصلاح الأخطاء أو الترجمة. يجب مراجعة التغييرات واعتمادها وفق GOV-001 قبل نشر إصدار تشغيلي.

### Using forms / استخدام النماذج

**English:** Use the application record as the primary source wherever functionality exists. The attached FRM templates are PROPOSED MANUAL CONTROLS for approvals, physical verification, external evidence and exceptions; they are not proof the application has those screens. Do not duplicate sensitive personal or financial details unnecessarily. Form owners, signatories, retention and storage remain TBC.

**العربية:** استخدم سجل النظام الأساسي كلما توفرت الوظيفة. نماذج FRM المرفقة ضوابط يدوية مقترحة للموافقات والفحص الفعلي والإثباتات الخارجية والاستثناءات؛ ولا تعني وجود شاشات مماثلة في التطبيق. تجنب تكرار البيانات الشخصية والمالية الحساسة دون حاجة. مسؤوليات الحفظ والتوقيع والاحتفاظ غير معتمدة.

### Stop and escalate / متى تتوقف وتصعّد

**English:** Stop and inform the designated owner for wrong account/academy, unresolved horse welfare or rider safety concern, missing professional clearance, unverified money, stock expiry, date locks, privacy exposure or denied server validation. Never bypass a restriction with direct database changes or a demo reset.

**العربية:** أوقف التنفيذ وأبلغ المسؤول المخول عند ظهور حساب أو أكاديمية خاطئة، أو مشكلة سلامة أو رفاه خيل، أو غياب تصريح مهني، أو أموال غير مثبتة، أو مخزون منتهي الصلاحية، أو قفل تاريخي، أو تسرب خصوصية، أو رفض الخادم للعملية. لا تتجاوز القيود بتعديل مباشر للبيانات أو إعادة ضبط تجريبية.

## MANAGEMENT DECISIONS / القرارات الإدارية المعلقة

- **DEC-01** Registered legal/operator name, address and actual operating services — Document authority, contracts and customer communications; Proposed approver: Club owner/legal

- **DEC-02** Current organisation chart and named deputies/escalation contacts — Clear responsibility and absence cover; Proposed approver: Club management

- **DEC-03** Operating hours, shifts, opening/closing and handover cadence — Timely work and exception ownership; Proposed approver: Operations

- **DEC-04** Member eligibility, age/guardian consent and identity verification — Admission and safeguarding; Proposed approver: Management/qualified advisers

- **DEC-05** Rider experience/fitness assessment and suitable horse/instructor allocation — Safe session decisions beyond software availability; Proposed approver: Head instructor/clinical authority

- **DEC-06** PPE/tack inspection standards, facility/arena readiness and supervision limits — Operational safety; not implied by class capacity; Proposed approver: Qualified operations/safety lead

- **DEC-07** Weather/heat/lightning/visibility stop-resume criteria — Local operating risk; no thresholds supplied; Proposed approver: Qualified safety/operations lead

- **DEC-08** Medical/veterinary authority, prescribed plans and clearance evidence — Prevent unauthorised treatment or return to work; Proposed approver: Veterinary lead/management

- **DEC-09** Individual feeding/water instructions and permitted substitutions — Demo quantities are not real rations; Proposed approver: Veterinary/horse-care authority

- **DEC-10** Horse intake, disease/quarantine, handling and transport requirements — Welfare and physical handover; Proposed approver: Veterinary/stable lead

- **DEC-11** Current prices, package eligibility and approved promotions — Avoid using synthetic rates or assumptions; Proposed approver: Finance/management

- **DEC-12** Commercial cancellation, no-show, late arrival, reschedule and makeup rules — System permits certain operations but does not define every customer entitlement; Proposed approver: Management/finance/legal

- **DEC-13** Package expiry/transfer/freeze rules, if any — Current software has no automatic expiry; policy must not promise unsupported automation; Proposed approver: Management/finance

- **DEC-14** Refund authorisation limits, evidence and segregation of duties — Permission to enter a refund is not approval to pay one; Proposed approver: Finance/management

- **DEC-15** Official invoicing provider, tax workflow and reconciliation ownership — Operational pro formas are not tax invoices; Proposed approver: Finance/qualified tax adviser

- **DEC-16** Boarding contract, inclusions, period pricing, renewals, departure and notice rules — Current software charges a selected amount once per stay; Proposed approver: Stable manager/finance/legal

- **DEC-17** Inventory units, suppliers, ordering, receipt inspection, counts and variance approvals — Forecasts do not replace physical stock control; Proposed approver: Stores/finance

- **DEC-18** Communication response times, languages, complaint ownership and mailbox monitoring — A queued notification is not a completed service response; Proposed approver: Member services/management

- **DEC-19** Password standard, onboarding channel, access-review/offboarding timing — Technical minimum is not sufficient business security policy; Proposed approver: IT/data owner

- **DEC-20** Retention, privacy requests, deletion/anonymisation evidence and incident reporting duties — System preserves some financial/service history; Proposed approver: Legal/data owner

- **DEC-21** Backup frequency, encryption/location, retention, RPO/RTO and restore-test schedule — Recovery cannot be assumed from an untested file; Proposed approver: IT/system owner

- **DEC-22** System outage, emergency contacts, temporary paper operations and recovery reconciliation — Staff need a controlled fallback; Proposed approver: Operations/IT

- **DEC-23** Printed planner signatures, transcription and original-paper custody — Paper completion does not update digital records; Proposed approver: Operations/data owner

- **DEC-24** Which data is live versus demo, current import completion and media approvals — Prevent fictional records entering customer/clinical decisions; Proposed approver: System owner/operations

- **DEC-25** Current release/deployment versions, store availability and supported device QA — Source implementation is not proof of a working deployment; Proposed approver: Release/system owner

- **DEC-26** Signed approval of Arabic terminology and bilingual customer wording — Avoid inconsistent or misleading commitments; Proposed approver: Management/language reviewer
